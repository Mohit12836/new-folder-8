## Industry Solutions for Wood Industry

At Rays International Industrial Solutions, we offer high-quality products specifically designed to meet the demanding needs of the wood industry. Our comprehensive range of solutions ensures optimal performance, durability, and efficiency in wood processing and manufacturing.

#### Our Products for the Wood Industry

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Socket-Screws.jpg)

### Nut Bolt Products

Our nut and bolt products are engineered to withstand the high stresses and dynamic conditions typical in wood processing operations. Made from premium materials, these fasteners provide exceptional resistance to wear and corrosion, ensuring the structural integrity and reliability of your equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/orings.jpg)

### Seals & O Ring Products

We offer a variety of seals and O-rings designed to prevent leaks and maintain the integrity of wood processing machinery. Available in custom sizes and high-durability materials, our sealing solutions ensure reliable performance and long-lasting durability even under harsh conditions.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/THK-Lubrication.jpg)

### Lubrication Oil & Grease Products

Our lubrication oils and greases are formulated to reduce friction and wear on wood processing machinery, ensuring smooth and efficient operation. These high-performance lubricants help extend equipment lifespan, reduce maintenance costs, and enhance overall productivity.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/6.jpg)

### Rubber Emery/Roller Covering Products

Our rubber emery and roller coverings are designed to provide superior performance and durability in wood processing equipment. These coverings ensure efficient operation, reduce downtime, and enhance the longevity of your machinery, contributing to a more reliable production process.

#### Applications in the Cement Industry

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Sawmilling-and-Cutting.jpg)

##### Sawmilling and Cutting

Our high-strength fasteners, reliable seals, and effective lubricants contribute to the optimal performance of sawmilling and cutting equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Planing-and-Shaping.jpg)

##### Planing and Shaping

Durable seals and lubrication products ensure the efficient and safe operation of planing and shaping machines.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Finishing-and-Coating-1.jpg)

##### Finishing and Coating

Our nut bolt and sealing solutions help maintain the structural integrity and precision of finishing and coating equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Material-Handling-8.jpg)

##### Material Handling

From conveyor systems to handling equipment, our products ensure smooth and safe operations throughout the wood processing production process.
