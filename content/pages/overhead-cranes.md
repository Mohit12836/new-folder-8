## Industry Solutions for Overhead Cranes

At Rays International Industrial Solutions, we offer high-quality products specifically designed to meet the demanding needs of overhead crane operations. Our comprehensive range of solutions ensures optimal performance, safety, and durability in lifting and material handling applications.

#### Our Products for the Cement Industry

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Petrochemical-Studbolts.jpg)

### Nut Bolt Products

Our nut and bolt products are engineered to withstand the heavy loads and dynamic stresses typical in overhead crane operations. Made from premium materials, these fasteners provide exceptional resistance to wear and corrosion, ensuring the structural integrity and safety of your crane systems.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/O-Ring-Feature.jpg)

### Seals & O Ring Products

We offer a variety of seals and O-rings designed to prevent leaks and maintain the integrity of overhead crane machinery. Available in high-durability materials and custom sizes, our sealing solutions ensure reliable performance and long-lasting durability even under extreme conditions.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Kluber-Lubrication.jpg)

### Lubrication Oil & Grease Products

Our lubrication oils and greases are formulated to reduce friction and wear on crane machinery, ensuring smooth and efficient operation. These high-performance lubricants help extend equipment lifespan, reduce maintenance costs, and enhance overall productivity.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/6.jpg)

### Rubber Emery/Roller Covering Products

Our rubber emery and roller coverings are designed to provide superior performance and durability in crane applications. These coverings ensure efficient operation, reduce downtime, and enhance the longevity of your crane systems, contributing to a more reliable material handling process.

#### Applications in the Cement Industry

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Lifting-and-Hoisting.jpg)

##### Lifting and Hoisting

Our high-strength fasteners, reliable seals, and effective lubricants contribute to the optimal performance of lifting and hoisting equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Trolley-and-Bridge-Motion.jpg)

##### Trolley and Bridge Motion

Durable seals and lubrication products ensure the efficient and safe operation of trolley and bridge motions.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Safety-Mechanisms.jpg)

##### Safety Mechanisms

Our nut bolt and sealing solutions help maintain the structural integrity and precision of safety mechanisms.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Material-Handling-7.jpg)

##### Material Handling

From conveyor systems to handling equipment, our products ensure smooth and safe operations throughout the crane's material handling process.
