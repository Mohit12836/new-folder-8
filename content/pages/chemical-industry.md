## Industry Solutions for Chemical Industry

At Rays International Industrial Solutions, we specialize in delivering high-quality products tailored to the unique demands of the chemical industry. Our comprehensive range of solutions ensures optimal performance, safety, and efficiency in chemical processing and production.

#### Our Products for the Chemical Industry

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Socket-Screws.jpg)

### Nut Bolt Products

Our nut and bolt products are designed to withstand the corrosive environments and high pressures typical in the chemical industry. Made from premium materials, these fasteners provide exceptional resistance to chemical reactions, ensuring the structural integrity and safety of your equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/O-ringen.jpg)

### Seals & O Ring Products

We offer a variety of seals and O-rings engineered to prevent leaks and maintain the integrity of chemical processing equipment. Our sealing solutions are available in custom sizes and materials to handle the aggressive chemicals and high temperatures encountered in the industry, ensuring reliable and long-lasting performance.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/THK-Lubrication.jpg)

### Lubrication Oil & Grease Products

Our lubrication oils and greases are formulated to reduce friction and wear on machinery, even in the harshest chemical environments. These high-performance lubricants help maintain smooth and efficient operation, extend equipment lifespan, and minimize maintenance downtime, enhancing overall productivity.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/3.jpg)

### Rubber Emery/Roller Covering Products

Our rubber emery and roller coverings are designed to provide superior durability and chemical resistance in processing equipment. These coverings ensure efficient operation, reduce downtime, and enhance the longevity of your machinery, contributing to a more reliable and safe production process.

#### Applications in the Chemical Industry

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Processing-and-Mixing.jpg)

##### Processing and Mixing

Our high-strength fasteners, reliable seals, and effective lubricants contribute to the optimal performance of processing and mixing equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Pumping-Systems.jpg)

##### Pumping Systems

Durable seals and lubrication products ensure the efficient and safe operation of pumps handling various chemicals.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Storage-Tanks.jpeg)

##### Storage Tanks

Our nut bolt and sealing solutions help maintain the structural integrity and leak-proof performance of storage tanks.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Material-Handling-1.jpg)

##### Material Handling

From conveyor systems to chemical handling equipment, our products ensure smooth and safe operations throughout the production process.
