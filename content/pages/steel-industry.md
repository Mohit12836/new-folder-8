## Industry Solutions for Steel Industry

#### Our Products for the Cement Industry

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Hex-Nuts.jpg)

### Nut Bolt Products

Our nut and bolt products are engineered to withstand the extreme conditions and heavy loads typical in steel manufacturing. Made from premium materials, these fasteners provide exceptional resistance to heat, wear, and corrosion, ensuring the structural integrity and reliability of your equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Seals-O-Ring.jpg)

### Seals & O Ring Products

We offer a variety of seals and O-rings designed to prevent leaks and maintain the integrity of steel processing machinery. Available in high-temperature-resistant materials and custom sizes, our sealing solutions ensure reliable performance even under the most demanding conditions.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Shell-Lubrication.jpeg)

### Lubrication Oil & Grease Products

Our lubrication oils and greases are formulated to reduce friction and wear on steel machinery, ensuring smooth and efficient operation. These high-performance lubricants help extend equipment lifespan, reduce maintenance costs, and enhance overall productivity.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/3.jpg)

### Rubber Emery/Roller Covering Products

Our rubber emery and roller coverings are designed to provide superior performance and durability in steel processing equipment. These coverings ensure efficient operation, reduce downtime, and enhance the longevity of your machinery, contributing to a more reliable production process.

#### Applications in the Steel Industry

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Melting-and-Casting.jpg)

##### Melting and Casting

Our high-strength fasteners, reliable seals, and high-temperature lubricants contribute to the optimal performance of melting and casting equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Rolling-and-Forming.jpg)

##### Rolling and Forming

Durable seals and lubrication products ensure the efficient and safe operation of rolling mills and forming equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Finishing-and-Coating-2.jpg)

##### Finishing and Coating

Our nut bolt and sealing solutions help maintain the structural integrity and precision of finishing and coating equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Material-Handling-11.jpg)

##### Material Handling

From conveyor systems to handling equipment, our products ensure smooth and safe operations throughout the steel production process.
