## Lubrication Oil & Grease

#### Premium Lubrication Solutions

AtRays International Industrial Solutions, we are committed to offering the highest quality lubrication products to ensure the smooth and efficient operation of your machinery and equipment. We proudly supply lubrication oils and greases from leading brands such asKluber,THK, andShell, known for their superior performance and reliability.

#### Kluber Lubrication

- Overview:Kluber is renowned for its innovative and high-performance lubrication solutions. With a focus on sustainability and reliability, Kluber products are designed to meet the specific needs of various industries, including automotive, food processing, and heavy machinery.
- Products:High-temperature greases, synthetic lubricants, specialty oils, and maintenance lubricants.
- Benefits:Extended equipment life, reduced maintenance costs, and enhanced operational efficiency.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Kluber-Lubrication.jpg)

#### THK Lubrication

- Overview:THK is a leader in the field of linear motion systems, offering a range of lubrication solutions that ensure precision and durability. THK lubricants are engineered to meet the rigorous demands of advanced machinery and automation systems.
- Products:Linear motion greases, ball screw lubricants, and high-load bearing oils.
- Benefits:Improved precision, reduced wear and tear, and consistent performance under high-load conditions.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/THK-Lubrication.jpg)

#### Shell Lubrication

- Overview:Shell is a globally recognized brand, providing a comprehensive range of lubrication products that cater to various industrial applications. Known for their reliability and advanced technology, Shell lubricants help enhance the efficiency and lifespan of equipment.
- Products:Industrial oils, greases, hydraulic fluids, and transmission oils.
- Benefits:Superior protection against friction, oxidation stability, and excellent performance in extreme conditions.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Shell-Lubrication.jpeg)
