## Industry Solutions for Food Industry

At Rays International Industrial Solutions, we are dedicated to providing high-quality products specifically tailored to meet the stringent requirements of the food industry. Our comprehensive range of solutions ensures optimal performance, hygiene, and efficiency in food processing and production.

#### Our Products for the Food Industry

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Structural-Assemblies.jpg)

### Nut Bolt Products

Our nut and bolt products are designed to meet the strict hygiene and safety standards of the food industry. Made from food-grade materials, these fasteners provide exceptional resistance to corrosion and contamination, ensuring the structural integrity and safety of your equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/static-seal_0011.jpg)

### Seals & O Ring Products

We offer a variety of seals and O-rings engineered to prevent leaks and maintain the integrity of food processing equipment. Available in food-safe materials and custom sizes, our sealing solutions ensure reliable performance and compliance with food safety regulations.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/orings.jpg)

### Lubrication Oil & Grease Products

Our lubrication oils and greases are formulated to reduce friction and wear on machinery while meeting the food industry’s stringent safety standards. These high-performance lubricants help maintain smooth and efficient operation, extend equipment lifespan, and ensure contamination-free production.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/4.jpg)

### Rubber Emery/Roller Covering Products

Our rubber emery and roller coverings are designed to provide superior performance and hygiene in food processing equipment. These coverings ensure efficient operation, reduce downtime, and enhance the longevity of your machinery, contributing to a more reliable and safe production process.

#### Applications in the Food Industry

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Processing-and-Mixing-1.jpg)

##### Processing and Mixing

Our high-strength fasteners, reliable seals, and food-grade lubricants contribute to the optimal performance of food processing and mixing equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Conveying-Systems.jpg)

##### Conveying Systems

Durable seals and lubrication products ensure the efficient and safe operation of conveyor systems handling various food products.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Packaging-Equipment.jpg)

##### Packaging Equipment

Our nut bolt and sealing solutions help maintain the structural integrity and leak-proof performance of packaging equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Material-Handling-2.jpg)

##### Material Handling

From conveyors to handling equipment, our products ensure smooth, hygienic, and safe operations throughout the food production process.
