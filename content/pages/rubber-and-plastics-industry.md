## Industry Solutions for Rubber and Plastics Industry

At Rays International Industrial Solutions, we provide high-quality products specifically designed to meet the rigorous demands of the rubber and plastics industry. Our comprehensive range of solutions ensures optimal performance, durability, and efficiency in manufacturing and processing operations.

#### Our Products for the Cement Industry

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Hex-Bolts-Screws.jpg)

### Nut Bolt Products

Our nut and bolt products are engineered to withstand the high pressures and dynamic stresses typical in rubber and plastics manufacturing. Made from premium materials, these fasteners offer exceptional resistance to wear, corrosion, and high stress, ensuring the structural integrity and reliability of your equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/O-ringen.jpg)

### Seals & O Ring Products

We offer a variety of seals and O-rings designed to prevent leaks and maintain the integrity of rubber and plastics processing machinery. Available in high-durability materials and custom sizes, our sealing solutions ensure reliable performance and long-lasting durability even under extreme conditions.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/THK-Lubrication.jpg)

### Lubrication Oil & Grease Products

Our lubrication oils and greases are formulated to reduce friction and wear on machinery, ensuring smooth and efficient operation. These high-performance lubricants help extend equipment lifespan, reduce maintenance costs, and enhance overall productivity.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/6.jpg)

### Rubber Emery/Roller Covering Products

Our rubber emery and roller coverings are designed to provide superior performance and durability in rubber and plastics processing equipment. These coverings ensure efficient operation, reduce downtime, and enhance the longevity of your machinery, contributing to a more reliable production process.

#### Applications in the Rubber and Plastics Industry

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Mixing-and-Compounding.jpg)

##### Mixing and Compounding

Our high-strength fasteners, reliable seals, and effective lubricants contribute to the optimal performance of mixing and compounding equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Molding-and-Extrusion.jpg)

##### Molding and Extrusion

Durable seals and lubrication products ensure the efficient and safe operation of molding and extrusion machines.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Finishing-and-Assembly.jpg)

##### Finishing and Assembly

Our nut bolt and sealing solutions help maintain the structural integrity and precision of finishing and assembly equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Material-Handling-12.jpg)

##### Material Handling

From conveyor systems to handling equipment, our products ensure smooth and safe operations throughout the rubber and plastics production process.
