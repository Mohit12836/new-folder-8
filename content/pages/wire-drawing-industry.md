## Industry Solutions for Wire Drawing Industry

At Rays International Industrial Solutions, we offer high-quality products specifically designed to meet the demanding requirements of the wire drawing industry. Our comprehensive range of solutions ensures optimal performance, durability, and efficiency in wire drawing operations.

#### Our Products for the Cement Industry

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Structural-Assemblies.jpg)

### Nut Bolt Products

Our nut and bolt products are engineered to withstand the high stresses and dynamic conditions typical in wire drawing operations. Made from premium materials, these fasteners provide exceptional resistance to wear and corrosion, ensuring the structural integrity and reliability of your equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/rubber-gaskets.jpg)

### Seals & O Ring Products

We offer a variety of seals and O-rings designed to prevent leaks and maintain the integrity of wire drawing machinery. Available in custom sizes and high-durability materials, our sealing solutions ensure reliable performance and long-lasting durability even under extreme conditions.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Shell-Lubrication.jpeg)

### Lubrication Oil & Grease Products

Our lubrication oils and greases are formulated to reduce friction and wear on wire drawing machinery, ensuring smooth and efficient operation. These high-performance lubricants help extend equipment lifespan, reduce maintenance costs, and enhance overall productivity.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/3.jpg)

### Rubber Emery/Roller Covering Products

Our rubber emery and roller coverings are designed to provide superior performance and durability in wire drawing equipment. These coverings ensure efficient operation, reduce downtime, and enhance the longevity of your machinery, contributing to a more reliable production process.

#### Applications in the Wire Drawing Industry

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Drawing-Machines.jpg)

##### Drawing Machines

Our high-strength fasteners, reliable seals, and effective lubricants contribute to the optimal performance of wire drawing machines.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Annealing-and-Coating.jpg)

##### Annealing and Coating

Durable seals and lubrication products ensure the efficient and safe operation of annealing and coating equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Spooling-and-Winding.jpg)

##### Spooling and Winding

Our nut bolt and sealing solutions help maintain the structural integrity and precision of spooling and winding equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Material-Handling-9.jpg)

##### Material Handling

From conveyor systems to handling equipment, our products ensure smooth and safe operations throughout the wire drawing production process.
