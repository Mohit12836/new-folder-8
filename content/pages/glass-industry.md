## Industry Solutions for Glass Industry

At Rays International Industrial Solutions, we provide high-quality products specifically designed to meet the demanding requirements of the glass industry. Our comprehensive range of solutions ensures optimal performance, durability, and efficiency in glass manufacturing and processing.

#### Our Products for the Glass Industry

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Hex-Nuts.jpg)

### Nut Bolt Products

Our nut and bolt products are engineered to withstand the high temperatures and demanding conditions of the glass industry. Made from premium materials, these fasteners provide exceptional resistance to heat and corrosion, ensuring the structural integrity and reliability of your equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/O-Ring-Feature.jpg)

### Seals & O Ring Products

We offer a variety of seals and O-rings designed to prevent leaks and maintain the integrity of glass manufacturing equipment. Available in high-temperature-resistant materials and custom sizes, our sealing solutions ensure reliable performance even under extreme conditions.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Shell-Lubrication.jpeg)

### Lubrication Oil & Grease Products

Our lubrication oils and greases are formulated to reduce friction and wear on machinery, ensuring smooth operation in the high-temperature environment of glass production. These high-performance lubricants help extend equipment lifespan, reduce maintenance costs, and enhance overall productivity.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/5-1.jpg)

### Rubber Emery/Roller Covering Products

Our rubber emery and roller coverings are designed to provide superior performance and durability in glass processing equipment. These coverings ensure efficient operation, reduce downtime, and enhance the longevity of your machinery, contributing to a more reliable production process.

#### Applications in the Glass Industry

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Melting-and-Forming.jpg)

##### Melting and Forming

Our high-strength fasteners, reliable seals, and high-temperature lubricants contribute to the optimal performance of melting and forming equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Annealing-and-Tempering.jpg)

##### Annealing and Tempering

Durable seals and lubrication products ensure the efficient and safe operation of annealing and tempering ovens.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Cutting-and-Finishing.jpg)

##### Cutting and Finishing

Our nut bolt and sealing solutions help maintain the structural integrity and precision of cutting and finishing equipment.

![](https://raysinternational.net/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Material-Handling-3.jpg)

##### Material Handling

From conveyors to handling equipment, our products ensure smooth and safe operations throughout the glass production process.
