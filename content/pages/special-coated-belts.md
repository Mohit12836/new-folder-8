#### Application-Specific Coated Belts

At Rays International Industrial Solutions, we supply special coated belts engineered for specific handling needs. With coatings such as sponge, PU, rubber and studded surfaces, these belts provide enhanced grip, cushioning and product protection for specialised conveying and drive applications.
