#### Mounted Bearing Units

At Rays International Industrial Solutions, we offer a complete range of pillow block bearings — bearing units housed in a mounted block for easy installation and support of rotating shafts. Robust and simple to maintain, they are ideal for conveyors, fans and a wide variety of industrial equipment.
